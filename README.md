## How To Run

1. Download the `.exe` from the latest release.
2. Place it anywhere you like. The exe is fully portable, and your projects live in your **Documents** folder, not next to the exe.
3. Double-click to launch. No installer.
4. On the first run, Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unsigned executable. Choose **More info** then **Run anyway** to launch.

## Where Your Files Live

On first launch, RIMSET creates this layout under your Documents folder:

```
C:\Users\<you>\Documents\RIMSET\
├── Standalone Projects\     (single-asset .apdproj files)
├── Mod Projects\            (full mod folders)
├── Exported Mods\           (drop these into RimWorld\Mods\)
└── Exported Assets\         (raw PNG exports)
```

You can move the exe around freely without losing anything. To back up your work, copy the whole `RIMSET` folder.

## What You Need

* Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
* About 200 MB of free disk space (portable version unpacks in cache)
